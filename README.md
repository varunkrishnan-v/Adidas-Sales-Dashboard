# 👟 Adidas Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Adidas Sales Dashboard** is an interactive sales analysis project designed to evaluate Adidas product performance across different **products, regions, states, and sales methods**.

The dashboard provides a clear view of key business metrics such as **Total Sales, Units Sold, Profit Margin, Product Performance, and Sales Method Performance**. Interactive slicers allow users to explore the data and identify important sales trends and business opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall Adidas sales performance.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories.
- Compare sales generated through different sales methods.
- Evaluate product-wise operating profit.
- Understand regional and state-level sales performance.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Men's Street Footwear** is the highest-performing product category, generating approximately **$20.9M in sales**.
- **Women's Apparel** is the second-largest contributor with approximately **$17.8M in sales**.
- **In-store sales** contribute the largest share of total sales at approximately **39.71%**.
- **Outlet sales** account for approximately **32.72%** of sales.
- **Online sales** contribute approximately **27.57%**, making them the smallest of the three sales channels in this dataset.
- Men's Street Footwear performs strongly across multiple sales methods, making it an important product segment.
- Product performance varies considerably, indicating opportunities for targeted product and channel strategies.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Collection
The Adidas sales dataset was imported into Microsoft Excel.

### 2. Data Cleaning
The dataset was checked for:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Incorrect data types
- Formatting inconsistencies

### 3. Data Preparation
Relevant fields were prepared for analysis, including:

- Product
- Region
- State
- Sales Method
- Total Sales
- Units Sold
- Operating Profit
- Profit Margin

### 4. Data Analysis
Pivot Tables were created to summarize sales and profitability across different business dimensions.

### 5. Dashboard Development
Pivot Charts, KPI cards, slicers, and visual elements were combined to create an interactive Excel dashboard.

### 6. Business Analysis
The final dashboard was analyzed to identify high-performing products, sales channels, and business trends.
